Story

From running a café to running attrition models

Karan didn't start in HR. He trained in hospitality at IHM Bangalore, then opened Café 221B in Pune — a small business that taught him, fast, what it actually takes to keep a team showing up and a customer coming back.

A stint in field sales at Whirlpool followed, then an MBA at FLAME University to put structure around what he'd learned about people and performance. Since 2020, that combination has played out at Bajaj Finance — first as a Management Trainee in Collections, now as a Senior Unit Manager and HR Business Partner across the collections and legal functions.

The work spans the full lifecycle: performance appraisals for thousands of employees, training frameworks that reach the field, redeployment projects that reshape teams, and an Early Warning System that flags attrition risk before it becomes a resignation letter.

"HR isn't a support function when you treat it like a system — something you can measure, predict, and improve."
